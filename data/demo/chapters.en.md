# The Van Aliquam – De Loremans family

## Sluis, around 1800

[Joannes Van Aliquam](/en/person/joannes-van-aliquam/) was born in 1789. He and [Catharina De Loremans](/en/person/catharina-de-loremans/) raised a family near Sluis. Their children were Pieter, Margaretha and Cornelis. Cornelis died in infancy. Pieter became a farmer and married Johanna Verdolor, whose father Wouter was the village baker of Sluis. Every register mentioned here is fictional.

## The twins, 1859

Pieter and Johanna had five children. [Maaike](/en/person/maaike-van-aliquam/) and [Marie](/en/person/marie-van-aliquam/) were twins, born on 12 June 1859. Maaike married the teacher Hendrik De Loremans and moved to Eeklo. Marie married the brewer Jacob Verdolor. Jacob's parents are unknown; a shared surname alone is no proof of a family link.

## A life along the canal

[Jan Van Aliquam](/en/person/jan-van-aliquam/) moved to Schoten in 1878, to the Kempen canal (Dessel-Schoten), and worked on the quays. His wife Sophie came from a family of lock keepers. Their son [Lodewijk](/en/person/lodewijk-van-aliquam/) married the teacher [Emma De Loremans](/en/person/emma-de-loremans/) in 1911. They stayed married until his death in 1974, more than sixty-two years later. Their children were the twins Lieve and Leen, Henri, and Karel. Lodewijk's sister Gerarda worked as a seamstress.

Jan's birthday illustrates conflicting evidence: the family book gives 12 March 1854, while the fictional civil record gives 14 March. His page follows the record and keeps both versions visible.

## Across the ocean, 1912

[Georges Van Nemo](/en/person/georges-van-nemo/), son of Ida Van Aliquam, married the seamstress [Anneke Dolorsit](/en/person/anneke-dolorsit/) in 1907. In 1912 they left for Detroit, where Georges found work in a brewery. An invented newspaper notice tells of their departure. Their son Harold and grandson Norbert later worked at the same brewery until it closed in 1979.

Anneke's brother [Felix](/en/person/felix-dolorsit/) moved to Antwerp and disappeared from the family's account. A possible death record has a matching initial and occupation, but no parents or other identifying details. It is listed as a possibility and has not been accepted as his death.

## The brewery and the classroom

[Henri Van Aliquam](/en/person/henri-van-aliquam/), Lodewijk and Emma's son, learned to brew in Sluis. A fictional newspaper says his homemade lemonade won a competition when he was ten. He later married Maria Ipsenbeke. Henri's younger brother [Karel](/en/person/karel-van-aliquam/) chose teaching and married his colleague Juliana De Loremans.

Some stories come only from [Griet De Loremans](/en/person/griet-de-loremans/). Those are labelled family story, rather than documented. Several guessed identities carry a question mark. The sources and confidence labels demonstrate the difference between a record, a plausible link and a remembered tale.

## Today

The youngest people have names and family links, with personal details hidden. Explore the [map](/en/map/), the [people list](/en/people/) or the [fun facts](/en/fun-facts/). All names, dates, records and family events are invented. The towns on the map are real.
